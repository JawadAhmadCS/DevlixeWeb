Freelancers often face significant challenges in calculating their taxes due to multiple income sources, varying regional tax regulations, and deductions. Traditional tax calculators were either too generalized or not designed for the unique needs of freelancers.

Gig Finance wanted Devlixe to create a solution that would simplify these calculations and provide precise estimates, improving the overall experience for freelancers.

No-Code Tax Calculator for Freelancers

The core of the solution is the interactive, no-code tax calculator built using Webflow. Unlike traditional software that requires technical expertise to use or maintain, this tool is designed to be completely user-friendly and accessible to freelancers, regardless of their technical skill level.

Real-Time Tax Deduction Feedback

One of the standout features we added to the tax calculator is real-time feedback on tax deductions. This means that as freelancers enter their income, deductions, and other relevant data, the tool immediately shows them the potential savings they could unlock.

Automated Updates for Changing Tax Laws

Tax laws are constantly evolving, and it can be a headache to keep up. That’s why we’ve implemented automated updates into the tax calculator. This system automatically reflects any changes in tax regulations, so freelancers don’t have to worry about manually updating the tool or missing important tax law changes.

The implementation of these solutions significantly improved the user experience and operational efficiency for Gig Finance.
