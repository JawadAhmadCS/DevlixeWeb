1. Introduction

Impleko LLC ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you subscribe to our SMS messaging services for appointment scheduling, reminders, rescheduling, and customer support.

2. Information We Collect

When you opt-in to our SMS messaging program, we collect:

  • Email Address: For appointment confirmations and support

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for the following purposes:

  • Scheduling and confirming appointments

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
